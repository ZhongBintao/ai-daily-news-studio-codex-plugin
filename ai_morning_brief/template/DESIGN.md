# AI 信号早报 · 晨光编辑部

The daily composition is a cool-white digital newsroom rather than a copy of
the supplied reference video.  It uses a deep ink-blue reading field, bright
signal blue, sunrise orange, and a restrained teal for data accents.  All
story claims are rendered from the AIHOT source package; the composition does
not fetch or invent photographs, logos, numbers, or source text.
